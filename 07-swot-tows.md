# Deliverable 7 — OurOwn SWOT & TOWS Analysis

**Specific to OurOwn:** owned-inventory grocery model, Kano-first launch, prototype-stage technology, operating under Afrizen Capital, October 2026. Every point carries evidence, confidence, commercial implication and response. Generic claims ("technology is a strength") are excluded.

**Confidence key:** H = high (official/primary read) · M = medium (credible secondary/corroborated) · L = low (indirect) · U = unverified.

## TOWS strategy matrix

| | **O** opportunities | **W/T** threats |
|---|---|---|
| **S** strengths | **SO (attack):** ① Use local sourcing (S1) + empty incumbent field (S2) to open with **market-price parity on staples** and a narrow 60–100 SKU list (O1/O7). ② Radio+Hausa+WhatsApp (S5) as launch channel (O4/O5). ③ Win B2B first (O1): bulk baskets fix density (T2) with prepaid invoices (W8). | **ST (defend):** ① S1 → survive T3 by weekly re-indexing prices against Singer/Dawanau. ② S2 → before Uber/Glovo arrive (T1), lock merchant & pickup-point exclusivity in core LGAs. ④ S4 → over-invest in repeat experience (S8 trust layer) to counter T4/T5. |
| **W** weaknesses | **WO (fix):** ① W1 → run Deliverable 11's 30-day validation before inventory spend (turns O1/O3 hypotheses into data). ② W2 → adopt the Deliverable 6 operating model (min basket ₦10k, batched ₦600, fee ≥₦800): turns loss-making orders into ₦300–700 contribution. ③ W3/W5 → 2-week legal + technical readiness sprint (Deliverables 8/9) before first real order. | **WT (avoid / minimise):** ① W4+T2 → zero owned fleet, rented delivery, staged capital, **no inventory debt** (Alerzo rule). ② W6+T1/T7 → one polygon, dual sourcing, explicit kill criteria (do not defend a losing geography: Wasoko cut Uganda/Zambia and survived; Copia didn't). ③ W7+T5 → ambient-only, prepaid-first, confirmation call — remove perishable+COD interaction entirely in phase 1. |

### Severity-ranked action list (top 6)

1. **Validate demand before capital** (W1×T2): 30-day plan; continue only if ≥8 of 30 interviews show recent pain + pilot clears 5 orders/day for 2 consecutive weeks at ≥₦10k AOV.
2. **Fix the margin model** (W2): blended ≥8–10%, minimum basket ₦10,000, fee structure per Deliverable 6 §3 — adopted as policy before launch.
3. **Prepaid-first + batched delivery** (W8×T5×T2): removes the two biggest cost leaks at once.
4. **Legal entity/objects + NDPC/FCCPC compliance-by-design** (W3×T6): one-week professional checklist.
5. **Price-index discipline** (S1×T3): weekly repricing, ≤7-day supplier quote validity.
6. **Density before geography** (W6×T1): contribution-positive for 3 months before Kaduna/Abuja conversations.
