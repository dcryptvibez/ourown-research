OurOwn — Market Research & Whitepaper Project

Evidence-based research on OurOwn, a proposed grocery and everyday-essentials commerce business launching in Kano, Northern Nigeria.

Research period: October 2026. All sources accessed: 28–30 October 2026 unless otherwise noted. Status of this work: Working research package for the founders of OurOwn / Afrizen Capital Limited. Not investment advice, not legal advice, not an audit.

Research plan (as executed)

  1. Desk research via multi-backend internet research (agent-reach routing: Exa web search, Jina Reader page fetch, direct official-source fetches), organised into ten parallel streams:
  2. A/O — Nigerian grocery & FMCG market size, structure, inflation, market-sizing inputs
  3. B — Kano & Northern Nigeria market validation (population, poverty, connectivity, retail fabric)
  4. C/M — customer behaviour, acquisition, retention, Kano media landscape
  5. D — competitor landscape incl. Glovo, Jumia, Chowdeck, Kano local players
  6. E — FMCG suppliers: Ghadco, Pop Cola/Mamuda, Kano wholesale markets
  7. H — last-mile delivery economics, 3PLs in Kano/Abuja, COD, cold chain
  8. I — payment providers (Paystack, Flutterwave, Monnify, Paga, PayGate)
  9. J — technology, security, outage and payment-confirmation evidence
  10. K — Nigerian legal/regulatory/tax/consumer obligations (CAC, NRS, FCCPC, NDPC, NAFDAC)
  11. L — case studies: GoLemon, Copia, Twiga, Alerzo, Wasoko/MaxAB, Jumia, Chowdeck, Blinkit, Getir
  12. Quantitative modelling — unit economics grids (4 basket sizes × 5 margin levels × 3 delivery-pricing strategies), sales-target decomposition, TAM/SAM/SOM bottom-up model. All formulas shown in the deliverables.
  13. Synthesis into 13 deliverables (below), with an evidence/confidence register.

Source rules applied: primary/official sources first; every major claim carries source, publisher, date and URL; snippets that could not be opened at source are flagged as such; conflicting figures are presented side-by-side rather than silently reconciled; nothing was invented — gaps are labelled as gaps and converted into primary-research tasks.

Headline caution (read first)

This research found no independent evidence of customer demand for OurOwn (no interviews, no live orders), no verified Kano e-grocery market data in any public source, and a category where the best-documented recent Nigerian entrant (GoLemon) shut down in July 2026 despite positive per-order contribution. The opportunity in Kano is real — no major delivery incumbent operates there — but the 5% gross-margin hypothesis and the ₦60m/₦100m sales targets are, on the modelled numbers, only viable under specific conditions set out in 13-decision-framework.md. Test before you build.
