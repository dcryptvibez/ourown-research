| Substitute | Wins on | Loses on | Implication for OurOwn |

## Part 5 — What OurOwn can offer that is meaningfully different

Not "more convenient or cheaper" — those are claims every failed entrant made. The defensible differences, each tied to evidence:

1. **No national platform serves Kano** (Glovo/PricePally city lists verified absent; Chowdeck unconfirmed) — a *local-first, Kano-native* operator with Hausa-language ordering and market-day scheduling.
2. **Landed-cost advantage**: buying at Dawanau/Singer/Ghadco in the same city where goods are made (Mamuda, NBC Challawa) vs Lagos-based competitors routing through Lagos middlemen.
3. **Ambient-first, batched, scheduled delivery** — designed for the actual economics (Deliverable 6) instead of importing 30-minute Lagos expectations that die on density.
4. **B2B institutional wedge** (schools, caterers, offices) — under-competed, prepaid, recurring (no public evidence anyone does this well in Kano).
5. **Trust layer**: fixed prices, receipts, replacement guarantee, named riders — the WhatsApp-commerce experience with accountability.
