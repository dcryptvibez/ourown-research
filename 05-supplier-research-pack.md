## 2. Direct-vs-open-market decision rule

Layer count costs 30–40% of the margin pool across the chain (DALA), **but** direct supply loads logistics, MOQ, deposits and 30–90-day cash cycles onto you (open market is cash-and-carry, "payment usually faster"). Professional norm:

> **For every SKU: compare (distributor price + transport + breakage + capital cost) vs (Singer/Dawanau carton price). Buy the lower landed cost. Track both weekly in the workbook (Deliverable 6, sheet 1).**

Practical priors: Kano-manufactured goods (Ghadco/Mamuda/OK Plast/NBC/7-Up) → go as direct as possible (freight ≈ 0). Multinational FMCG → expect appointed-distributor or market purchase, not ex-factory. Fresh/grains → Dawanau/Yankaba.

## 5. Supplier meeting questions (must-ask)

1. Exact price per carton/unit **today**, valid for how long?
2. MOQ and order frequency; who bears transport?
3. Payment: prepay, deposit, credit days? Bank transfer vs cheque?
4. **Returns:** what happens with short-dated (<3 months?) or damaged stock — credit, exchange, or nothing?
5. Promotional support: price-offs, free goods, POSM — how are claims paid and how fast? (Trade-claim speed is distributors' #1 complaint — BeatRoute.)
6. Stock consistency: which SKUs go out of stock during demand spikes (Ramadan, Sallah)?
7. Invoicing: will you issue VAT-compliant invoices? (Needed for OurOwn's tax records.)
8. Exclusivity/territory: any restriction on reselling online?
9. Who else in Kano do you supply? (Benchmark our price against theirs.)
